### Group 1

Without oceans, humans, animals and plants couldn't live. Most of the goods that we use are transported on ships by sea, and boats provide a way for people to travel making oceans important for both commerce (商业) and personal travel. The coast is also a place for people to live and to have fun. Many people like to spend their free time and holidays at the beach! Oceans provide 99% of the Earth's living space. The global ocean makes Earth a nice place for us all to live. We should do what we can to protect the ocean.
