###Group1
It's Sunday, July 5th. It's sunny. My family and I go for a picnic by the river. We bring food and a picnic mat and find a comfortable place to sit. Dad puts up the tent under the tree. Mom puts the food on the mat. My brother and I play by the river, catching little fish. That's interesting. We also see beautiful butterflies and colourful flowers. It is a wonderful day, and I love having a picnic by the river!

核心词表
	Sunday [ˈsʌndeɪ] n. 星期日
	释 the day of the week after Saturday and before Monday
	例 We're going to visit my grandparents on Sunday. 我们周日要去看望爷爷奶奶。

	释 the 7th month of the year
	例 I will have my summer vacation in July. 我七月放暑假。
	sunny [ˈsʌni] adj. 阳光充足的
	释 with a lot of bright light from the sun
	记 词根记忆：sun（阳光）+ n + y（……的）→阳光充足的
	例 It's sunny today. How about going boating in the park? 今天天气很好，去公园划船怎么样？
	picnic [ˈpɪknɪk] n. 野餐
	释 an occasion when people pack a meal and take it to eat outdoors, especially in the countryside
	例 We will go on a picnic this weekend. 这个周末我们要去野餐。
	river [ˈrɪvə(r)] n. 河，江
	释 a natural flow of water that continues in a long line across land to the sea/ocean
	记 联想记忆：一个司机（driver）掉进河（river）里，大声呼喊：“来人啊！（Come here）！”
	例 The Mississippi River is one of the longest rivers in the world. 密西西比河是世界上几大长河之一。

	例 We will put up five shelves in the yard. 我们将在院子里搭5个架子。

	释 a shelter made of a sheet of cloth which is used mainly by people who are camping
	例 Tents come in various shapes and sizes. 帐篷有各种各样的形状和大小。

	释 full of bright colours or having a lot of different colours
	例 The male birds are more colourful than the females. 这种鸟雄性比雌性更加色彩艳丽。
