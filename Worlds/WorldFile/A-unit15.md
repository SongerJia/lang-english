### Group 1

In October 2021, China's first five national parks were designated. They are home to nearly 30% of the key terrestrial (陆地的) wildlife species (物种) found in China, covering a protected land area of 230,000 square kilometres.

### Group 2
Biomass (生物质能) is energy that we get from plants around us. For example, deadwood (枯木) or grass is burned in power stations. This makes electricity (电). Biomass is green energy because we can always plant more new trees or crops after we use it. Biomass makes about 2% of the electricity we use. It has become an important part of our energy.

	释 a source of power, such as fuel, used for driving machines, providing heat, etc.
	例 an energy crisis 能源危机

	around [əˈraʊnd] prep./adv. 周围，四周；围绕

	example [ɪɡˈzɑːmpl] n. 例子，实例
	释 sth. such as an object, a fact or a situation that shows, explains or supports what you say
	记 词根记忆：ex（出）+ ampl（拿）+ e→拿出（好的）来→例子
	搭 give an example 举例
	take ... for example 以……为例
	例 Can you give me an example of what you mean? 你能给我举个例子说明你的意思吗？
	for example 例如
	grass [ɡrɑːs] n. 草，青草
	释 a common wild plant with narrow green leaves that are eaten by cows, horses, sheep, etc.
	记 联想记忆：班里（class）的同学在草地（grass）上野餐
	例 These grasses need water and sunshine to grow well. 这些草需要水和阳光才能长好。
	burn [bɜːn] vt./vi. 燃烧；点燃
	释 if you burn a fuel (燃料), or a fuel burns, it produces heat, light or energy
	记 联想记忆：飞蛾扑火→飞蛾围着火转（turn），然后被烧（burn）
	搭 burn down 烧毁 burn out 烧尽，燃尽
	例 My grandmother's family burns coal to cook. 我祖母家里通过烧煤来做饭。

	记 联想记忆：门前有3（three）棵树（tree）
	例 I liked climbing trees when I was about 10 years old. 我10岁左右时喜欢爬树。

生物质能是我们从周围的（around）植物中获得的能源（energy）。例如（for example），发电站（power station）燃烧（burn）枯木或草（grass），从而发电。生物质能是绿色能源，因为我们在使用后总是能种植更多新的（new）树木（tree）或作物（crop）。我们所用电能的2%由生物质能转化而来。生物质能已成为我们能源的重要组成部分。

### Group 3
A lot of the food we eat comes from the ocean, and millions of people around the world have jobs connected to fishing and fish farming. Much of our energy comes from the ocean—both oil and gas are found under the sea. Today we know that renewable energy (可再生能源) is better for the environment and we can make electricity from waves and tides. Oceans are really a reservoir (储存处) of resources (资源) for our mankind (人类).
