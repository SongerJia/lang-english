### Group 5

An American researcher who studied 600 millionaires found how rich you can get comes down to six wealth factors: thriftiness, confidence, responsibility, planning, focus and social indifference.

Being thrifty comes as no great surprise. Spending above your means, spending instead of saving for retirement, spending in anticipation of becoming wealthy, makes you a slave to the paycheck.

Having confidence is another key characteristic, as it helps people to be thrifty. It takes confidence to live within your means. It also takes confidence to invest properly. Instead of making investing decisions with your emotions, financial planners advise that you should leave your investments alone and focus on a long-term investment plan.

But people can’t invest or manage their own money without accepting responsibility for the outcomes. Many millionaires take on personal responsibility, and most also happen to be self-made, meaning they didn’t acquire their wealth through luck.

Millionaires focus on things they can control and align their daily habits to the goals they have set for themselves. They tend to be goal-oriented and hard workers, which enables them to plan financially and focus on seeing those plans through.

And it is these behaviours that make it easy for them to be socially indifferent. They resist lifestyle creep, the tendency to spend more whenever one earns more. Essentially, they don’t yield to pressure to buy the latest thing or to keep up with others or what they have acquired.

短文翻译

一位美国研究人员在对600名百万富翁（millionaire）进行研究后发现，你能变得多富有这件事可归结为（come down to）六个财富因素：节俭（thriftiness）、自信、责任、规划、专注和社交冷漠。

节俭（thrifty），这个因素并不令人惊讶。支出大于现有财富（means）、透支退休（retirement）储蓄、因预料到（in anticipation of）会变富而提前消费，这些都会让你成为工资（paycheck）的奴隶（slave）。

自信是另一个关键特征，因为自信有助于人们节俭。量入为出需要信心，正确投资也需要信心。理财规划师（planner）建议，与其带着情绪做投资决策，不如不做投资，而是专注于长期投资（investment）计划。

但是，如果不承担后果，人们就无法投资或管理自己的钱。许多百万富翁都承担起了个人责任，而且大多数都是白手起家（self-made），这意味着他们的财富不是靠运气得来的。

百万富翁专注于他们可以控制的事情，并使自己的日常习惯与他们为自己设定的目标保持一致（align）。他们往往以目标为导向（goal-oriented），勤奋工作，这使他们能够制定财务（financially）计划，并专注于坚持完成这些计划。

正是这些行为模式使他们很容易对社交漠不关心（indifferent）。他们拒绝（resist）生活方式的蠕变（creep），即收入越高、消费越多的倾向（tendency）。从根本上说（essentially），他们不会屈服于购买最新产品的压力，也不会去追赶其他人或是他们获得的东西。
