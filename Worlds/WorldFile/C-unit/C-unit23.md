### Group 1

Maybe you carry the most powerful passport in the world, or perhaps the rarest passport. Whatever the case, your passport will likely be one of four colours: red, blue, green or black, although there are slight variations. Switzerland’s passport is dark red, for example, while most European countries prefer a shade of red. That’s almost brown.

There are no official rules regarding passport colours, but countries follow a certain set of norms when designing them. The International Civil Aviation Organization suggests that countries use a suitable type, size and style for official documents like passports.

There are also a few compulsory aspects of passports. They must be made from a material that bends. They should be able to be read by a machine at temperatures ranging from 10 degrees Celsius to 50 degrees Celsius, under the conditions where the air can be extremely dry or moist.

Despite these existing recommendations, there is nothing regarding the cover colour. Why? Simply put, countries stick to darker and more conservative colours because they appear more official. And they can also hide dirt and wear.

Why should people remove their shoes before going inside their homes?

Bacteria are everywhere. They collect on your shoes when you walk along sidewalks, public toilet floors and even office carpets. Up to 421, 000 units of bacteria can collect on the outside of a single shoe, and it only takes a little bit of dirt to damage your timber floors. The more dirt you track in, the more it scratches the floor and the more often you have to clean. The extra scrubbing will harm your floors over time. Shoes can also leave marks and scratches on floors, especially high heels or shoes with pointy or hard parts. These can dent and scratch your floor. And if you live in an apartment building, removing your noisy shoes is the polite thing to do out of consideration for your downstairs neighbours. Walking around with bare feet is actually better for your feet. It strengthens the muscles in your feet. So many people are accustomed to wearing shoes during all their waking hours. The more time you spend wearing shoes, the more likely you are to incur foot injuries as a result.

短文翻译

停下片刻，想象一只蚊子（mosquito）正试图从你的手臂上吸（suck）血。你会做何反应？你一定（surely）会把蚊子拍（swat）飞。人类可以立即做出反应，避免危险情况的发生，植物也可以做到这一点。也许看起来不那么明显（obvious），但植物也有各种防御措施来保护自己免受攻击。这一点非常重要，因为植物是地球上生命的重要组成部分。它们不仅为我们提供食物、药物和木材，还为其他生物提供家园，制造我们呼吸的氧气，保持土壤肥沃，调节（regulate）湿度（humidity），促进气候稳定。

植物有许多天敌，从细菌到昆虫，从真菌到哺乳动物（mammal）。这些天敌被植物吸引，因为植物可能有鲜艳的颜色、芳香的气味、美味的叶子或果实。

与人类不同（unlike），植物无法逃离危险的捕食者。一些植物进化出了刺（thorn）和厚厚的叶片等保护（protective）结构，而另一些植物则产生防御性的（defensive）化学物质（chemical）。总之，植物的自我保护机制可以减少或防止植物捕食者可能造成的损害。
