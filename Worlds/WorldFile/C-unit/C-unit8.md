### Group 1

Studies have found obesity is associated with impairments in cognitive functioning, as assessed by a range of learning and memory tests, such as the ability to remember a list of words presented some minutes or hours earlier. There is also a growing body of evidence that diet-induced cognitive impairments can emerge rapidly—within weeks or even days. For example, one study found healthy adults assigned to a high-fat diet for five days showed impaired attention, memory, and mood compared with a low-fat diet control group. Body weight was not hugely different between the groups eating a healthy diet and those on high fat and sugar diets. Thus, body weight is not always the best indicator of health and a thin person still needs to eat well and exercise regularly.

After a tough workout or a day full of physical activity, it’s common to find your muscles aching, but where do these pains come from? Whether you’re just having a down day or a down period, taking a walk can instantly lift your mood, especially when you go outdoors. Not only can walking make you less depressed, but according to a new study, depression sufferers who took a daily walk showed just as much improvement in their symptoms as people taking medicine. In fact, 60-70 percent of the participants in the study could no longer even be classified as depressed.

Bone density may not be one of the most exciting health benefits of walking, but it’s an important one. People with stronger bones avoid osteoporosis and all the problems that come with it, like breaks and other disabilities. And the best way to get strong, healthy bones is by doing weight-bearing exercises like running, dancing and walking, according to a large study. But as regards bones, it’s definitely use it or lose it: to keep your bones strong, you have to keep exercising. The researchers found that adults who walked regularly had better bone density throughout their lives than their inactive friends.

Taking a walk can be great for clearing your head or blowing off some steam. It also provides a great opportunity to bond with friends and family, far away from electronics and other distractions at home. Even better, you set a powerful example because when they see you reaping the benefits of walking, they’ll be encouraged to walk more, too.

短文翻译

无论你是某天心情不好还是某段时间心情不好，散步都能立刻（instantly）改善你的情绪，尤其是去户外散步。散步不仅可以缓解你的抑郁情绪，而且根据一项新的研究，每天散步的抑郁症患者（sufferer）的症状（symptom）改善程度和吃药（medicine）的人一样。事实上，这个研究中有60%~70%的参与者甚至不再被界定为抑郁症患者。

骨密度（bone density）可能不是散步最令人激动的健康益处之一，但它是一个重要方面。骨骼强壮的人可以避免骨质疏松症及其所有并发症，比如骨折和其他残疾（disability）。一项大型研究表明，获得强壮、健康骨骼的最佳方式是进行诸如跑步、跳舞和散步等负重（weight-bearing）运动。但就骨骼而言（as regards），它绝对是用进废退：为了保持骨骼强壮，你必须坚持锻炼。研究人员发现，经常走路的成年人在一生中骨密度都要比他们不怎么活动的（inactive）朋友高。

散步可以很好地帮助你清理头脑或释放精力（blow off steam）。它还提供了一个与朋友和家人建立联系的好机会，能够远离电子产品（electronics）和家里的其他干扰。更棒的是，你树立了一个有力的榜样，因为当他们看到你从散步中获（reap）益时，他们也会受到鼓励，愿意多走路。
