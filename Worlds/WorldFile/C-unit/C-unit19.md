### Group 1

Regardless of which side of the argument you fall on, it seems likely that the use of AI in music production will only become more frequent. Our modern world is preoccupied with technological advancements. Instead of shying away from the idea of this bleak future, the best approach to take is one of optimism and curiosity. While there are always bound to be stubborn old-school musicians who refuse to use tech, music producers should consider AI as something to be embraced.

Imagine living without a fridge, a washing machine or a hoover, and getting stuff done without a laptop. Just 200 years ago that’s how people lived, but today it’s unthinkable: electrical and electronic appliances have made our life easier and richer, and now we can’t do without them. Yet, as much as we love and depend on them, we don’t seem to be as good at maintaining or disposing of them appropriately.

The global amount of uncollected e-waste has now reached 57 million tonnes.

The global figure for recycled e-waste is alarming: only about 17% is collected. Electrical waste in landfill is a problem because it contains toxic substances which can leak into the soil or lead to the depletion of the ozone layer.

An even more compelling reason to recycle e-waste is that it contains critical raw materials that can be reused, like gold, copper and palladium. This avoids the need to mine new material.

So it is critical that consumers recycle their old or broken equipment.

短文翻译

想象一下没有冰箱、洗衣机或吸尘器（hoover）的生活，以及不用笔记本电脑完成工作。在200年前，人们就是这样生活的，但今天这是不可想象的（unthinkable）：电器（electrical appliances）和电子器具（electronic appliances）让我们的生活变得更便捷丰富，现在我们已经离不开它们了。然而，尽管我们非常热爱和依赖它们，但我们似乎并不善于维护（maintain）或妥善（appropriately）处理（dispose of）它们。

目前，全球未收集的（uncollected）电子垃圾已达5700万吨。

全球回收（recycle）电子垃圾的数字令人震惊（alarming）：只有约17%被收集。垃圾填埋场（landfill）里的电子垃圾是一个问题，因为它含有有毒物质，可能渗入土壤或导致臭氧层（ozone layer）被破坏（depletion）。

回收电子垃圾的一个更有说服力的（compelling）理由是，电子废弃物中含有可重复使用的重要原材料，如金、铜（copper）和钯（palladium）。这样做就避免了开采（mine）新材料的需要。

因此，消费者回收废旧设备至关重要。
