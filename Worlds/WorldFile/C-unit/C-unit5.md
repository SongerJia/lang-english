### Group 1

The human thirst for knowledge is the driving force behind our successful development as a species. But curiosity can also be dangerous, leading to setbacks or even downfalls. Given curiosity’s complexity, scientists have found it hard to define.

While pinning down a definition has proven tricky, the general consensus is that curiosity is some means of information gathering. Psychologists also agree curiosity is intrinsically motivated.

Curiosity does have a genetic component. Genes and the environment interact in many complex ways to shape individuals and guide their behaviour, including their curiosity.

Regardless of their genetic makeup, infants have to learn an incredible amount of information in a short time, and curiosity is one of the tools humans have found to accomplish that gigantic task.

But curiosity often comes with a cost.

In some situations, the stakes are low and failure is a healthy part of growth. For instance, many babies are perfectly proficient crawlers. A study of 12- to 19-month-olds learning how to walk documented that these children fell down a lot. But walking is faster than crawling, so this motivates expert crawlers to transition to walking.

The university model needs to evolve. 短文翻译

文凭（diploma）是现代教育悲剧（tragedy）的完美结局（ending）。为什么？因为大学和课程（curriculum）的设计遵循了法国古典（classical）悲剧的三一律（unity）：时间、行动和地点的统一。学生们在20多岁时（时间的统一）在大学校园（地点的统一）上课（行动的统一）。这种传统模式按照惯例造就了名牌（prestigious）大学，但现在却受到了社会数字化（digitalization）的挑战——数字化让每个能上网的人都能获得学习资源；同时也受到现实需求的挑战——人们需要掌握（acquire）与瞬息万变的（fast-changing）世界同步（in step with）的技能。各高校必须认识到，仅仅在20多岁时学习是不够的。如果科技的（technological）传播（diffusion）和应用（implementation）发展得更快，劳动者就必须不断更新自己的技能。

高校需要改变模式。正如英国未来学家（futurist）理查德·沃森所说的那样，它必须让学生掌握（equip）正确的技能和知识，以便在未来世界中竞争（compete）——在这个世界中“价值主要（largely）来自人类互动，以及创造和理解机器所无法创造和理解的事情的能力”。通过教授基础（foundational）知识和最新技能，大学将为学生提供面向未来的终身学习（lifelong learning）技能，而不仅仅是让他们“为就业做好准备”。
